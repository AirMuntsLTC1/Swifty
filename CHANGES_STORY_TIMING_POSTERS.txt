CHANGES: video posters, story timing (15 s photos, 59 s video cap)
==================================================================

NO NEW MIGRATION. Run nothing new in Supabase for this version.

OPTIONAL (old videos may have a black poster stored):
  In Supabase SQL Editor, this clears stored posters so the app makes a
  new one from each video (taken a little way in, not the black first frame):
    update public.post_media set thumbnail_url = null where kind = 'video';

pubspec.yaml: no change (video_player, video_thumbnail, path_provider and
  visibility_detector were added in v3). Run flutter pub get anyway.

POSTERS
  lib/helper/video_thumbnail_helper.dart  frame taken ~20% in (min 0.5 s, max 3 s);
                                          posterFor() makes a poster from a video URL
  lib/widgets/video/video_poster.dart     NEW: stored thumbnail -> generated poster -> grey tile
  No black placeholders anywhere.

STORY TIMING
  Photos and text: 15 seconds.
  Video: the bar follows the video's own length; the next story starts when it ends.
  Videos longer than 59 seconds are cut at 59 seconds.
  Story composer picks videos up to 59 seconds.
  Long-press or reply box pauses the video and the bar.

PLAYER
  looping, paused, maxPlayback, onDuration, onPosition, onError parameters.
  Posts and feed: unchanged (loop, autoplay muted, tap opens thread).
